📋 Rules & How to Play

💸 How to Join

  1. Pay $20 via Venmo to @kendall_morton
  2. Watch the first episode (Tue Jun 2 · 8 pm CT on Peacock)
  3. Text Kendall your islander pick before Wed Jun 3 at 8 pm CT
  4. Bombshells who enter during episode 1 are eligible picks!

🏆 How to Win

  • The participant whose islander makes it the furthest wins the entire pot
  • If multiple people pick the winning islander, the pot is split equally
  • Keep that in mind when choosing — popular picks split the winnings!

🔀 Changing Your Pick

There are two windows to change your islander:

☝️
Before islanders return from Casa Amor
If your pick is sent home, you can buy back in with a new islander for $15 before the next episode airs. Kendall will text you.
✌️
During the 24-hour Casa Amor window
In the 24 hrs between the last Casa Amor episode and the islanders' return to the villa, you can switch your pick for $25. Kendall will send a reminder.

🔒 When Picks Lock

Picks are locked for the rest of the season when the islanders return from Casa Amor.

After that point, you cannot switch or buy back in — even if your islander gets sent home.
